# Open-source landscape for GrahmOS 2.0 (surveyed 2026-10-27)

**North star.** "Grandma's house": the place where things just work, where you can do anything,
even when the world outside is off. GrahmOS 2.0 is the operating layer that keeps that true:
the internet turned into a searchable, installable directory; the network kept functional with no
internet through phone-to-phone mesh and delay-tolerant physics; proven in a subway, where
everyone already understands that the signal dies.

Verdicts: **adopt** (use as a dependency or protocol now), **evaluate** (build a spike behind a
gate before committing), **watch** (track, do not build on yet), **skip** (does not fit the north
star, or licence/governance makes it unsafe). Licences are as reported by the project; verify
before distribution.

## What this means for GrahmOS 2.0

1. **Keep TerraNova's spine.** Signed envelopes, hash-chained logs, signed packs, the outbox and the relay are transport-agnostic and already proven. Everything below plugs into `Transport`.
2. **Add two transports and one protocol.** BLE on the bitchat wire format, Wi-Fi Aware for bulk, and RFC 9171 bundles (custody, lifetime) as the unit that crosses a subway.
3. **Make the directory a carried artefact.** ZIM for content, PMTiles for maps, signed packs for venues, MiniSearch on device and Tantivy on the edge. The "internet as a directory" is a catalogue of installable, verifiable packs.
4. **Prove it where the signal dies.** The subway acceptance test in `docs/SUBWAY_TEST.md` is the public demonstration; the SubwayMeshDTN model is its simulator.

Sources are linked inline; licence and activity claims come from the projects' own pages or package registries as of the survey date and must be re-verified before any dependency is added.
